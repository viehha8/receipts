How it works

Three steps. About thirty seconds.

1

Bring your chat

Export a WhatsApp or Telegram chat, paste messages in, or add the Receipts bot to your group.

2

Pick a time window

The whole chat, the last hour, or just last night’s planning session.

3

Get your receipts

Every plan sorted, every claim linked to its messages, plus a friendly message to settle anything that’s still open.

Confirmed

Someone said it and another person clearly agreed.

Not confirmed yet

Suggested or offered, but nobody else said yes.

Disputed

People disagreed and it was never settled.

Cancelled

It was agreed, then called off or replaced.

Built to be trusted

An AI that has to show its working

Receipts treats the AI as something that can be wrong or tricked, and checks its work in plain code.

  • Two-person rule

    Nothing is “confirmed” unless two different people are on record agreeing.

  • Every quote checked

    Each quote must match the real message word for word. Made-up ones are removed.

  • Tricks get caught

    Messages like “ignore your instructions” are flagged and can never count as agreement.

  • Private by default

    Phone numbers, emails, card numbers and NRICs are hidden before the AI reads anything. Pasted chats are never stored.

  • Forgets on purpose

    Telegram messages are deleted after 7 days. Share links expire after 30.
